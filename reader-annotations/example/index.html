Reading with a pencil

Whoever reads with a pencil in hand reads differently. Marking a sentence forces you to decide that the sentence matters, and that small decision is what turns reading into work. A text without marks is forgotten sooner than one where we have left a trace.

This page is an example. Select any fragment and a bar will appear with three actions: highlight, mark as important or add a note.

What deserves a mark

Not everything deserves the same treatment. Some sentences are worth being able to find again, and a few others, very few, carry the meaning of the whole document.

  • The yellow highlight is for what you want to find again.
  • The important mark is reserved for what you cannot afford to forget.
  • The note captures what the text makes you think, which is not always what the text says.

Going back to your marks

Marks are worth as much as the review. At the end of the page there is a list with everything you have highlighted, with filters and a button that takes you back to the exact spot in the text.

Everything is saved in this browser. If you want to take it to another device, use the copy and transfer button.
